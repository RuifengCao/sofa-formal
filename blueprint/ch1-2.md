## Conventions (Baek → Lean)
* Baek's moving sofa (Def 1.1.2) is *any translate* of a nonempty connected closed subset of
  `H_L` movable (by an SE(2) path) inside `L` into `V_L`.  Upstream `IsMovingSofa s m` fixes the
  translate (`s ⊆ H_L`, `m 0 = id`).  Bridge: `Sofa.IsSofaWithAngle S ω :=
  ∃ c m θ, IsMovingSofa (S + c) m ∧ θ continuous ∧ θ 0 = 0 ∧ m t = rot (θ t) + m t 0 ∧ θ 1 = -ω`.
* Rotation angle ω (Def 1.2.1 = 2.3.3): *clockwise* angle; in Lean `θ 1 = -ω` where `θ` is the
  continuous lift from `exists_rotationAngle` (`[PROOF-C]`).
* `S¹` angles are real numbers; `u t`, `v t`, `line`, `hpLe/hpLt/hpGe/hpGt` (Basic.lean).

**Proof of Thm 1.5.1 (verbatim structure, p.14).** Take any movement with angle ω ∈ ℝ, S initially in H_L.
(i) ω ≤ −π/4: IVT ⇒ a moment with clockwise angle −π/4; in S's frame S ⊆ H ∩ L′ with L′ a translate
of `R_{−π/4}`-rotated… (hallway rotated by π/4); `|H ∩ L′| = √2` (every horizontal slice of the
rotated L-shape has length √2) ⇒ contradiction with |S| ≥ 2.2.
(ii) |ω| < sec⁻¹2.2: final position ⇒ S ⊆ H ∩ (translate of V_ω), a parallelogram of area
sec ω < 2.2 — contradiction (this is `volume_le_of_forall_mem_strip`, Hammersley.lean).
Hence ω ≥ sec⁻¹2.2 (= 62.96°, > π/4).  (iii) If ω > π/2: IVT gives the moment with clockwise
angle exactly π/2; stop there and slide the sofa in +x direction until it touches `x = 1`
(its x-width is ≤ 1 because it started in H_L); sliding right keeps points of L in L, and the
end position lies in V_L.  Lean needs: path concatenation in `E(2)` (`Path.trans`), the √2 slice
lemma, the parallelogram lemma (exists), IVT (exists).
*Final assembly (Ch.1 p.14 + our M-H):* "By Theorem 1.5.1 and Gerver's sofa of area 2.2195… > 2.2
we can assume ω ∈ [sec⁻¹2.2, π/2]" — the paper uses |G| > 2.2 (numerical, cited); we use
Hammersley's sofa (`ofReal_two_point_two_lt_sofaConstant`, [PROOF-C-local]) instead.
